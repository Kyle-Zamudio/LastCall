# How to See the Email Notification Toggle

The code is already in the file, but your browser is showing a cached (old) version.

## Quick Fix: Hard Refresh

**Windows/Linux:**
- Press `Ctrl + F5` (or `Ctrl + Shift + R`)
- Or `Shift + F5`

**Mac:**
- Press `Cmd + Shift + R`

This forces the browser to reload the page and ignore the cache.

## Alternative: Clear Browser Cache

1. **Chrome/Edge:**
   - Press `F12` to open DevTools
   - Right-click the refresh button
   - Select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

2. **Or manually:**
   - Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
   - Select "Cached images and files"
   - Clear data

## Verify It's Working

After refreshing, you should see:

**In Business Settings:**
- A checkbox section with "📧 Email Notifications"
- Text: "Receive email alerts when workers express interest in your shifts"
- Located between "Menu Notes" and "Save Changes" button

**In Worker Settings (Edit Profile):**
- A checkbox section with "📧 Email Notifications"  
- Text: "Receive email alerts when new shifts matching your positions are posted"
- Located after age verification checkboxes
